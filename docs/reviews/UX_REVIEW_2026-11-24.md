## 1. The ten most important problems

**1. Raw internal errors reach the user, and two inputs crash the server.**
What the user sees:
- Typing "abc" in Shares and pressing Review shows `invalid number in order: [<class 'decimal.ConversionSyntax'>]`. The source is `exchange.py:383` and `:806`, which use `f"invalid number in order: {e}"`.
- Typing "abc" in the Screener's Min price shows `Screener filters are not valid: [object Object]`. FastAPI's 422 `detail` is a list, and `util.ts:9` does `new Error(j.detail)` on it.
- Creating an alert with price "abc" returns HTTP 500 ("Internal Server Error").
- POST /api/accounts with starting_cash "abc" also returns 500.
- With the server stopped, the ticket and the Fills tab both show "Failed to fetch".

Fixes:
- (a) In `util.ts` `api()`:
  - If `Array.isArray(j.detail)`, build the message from the items, e.g. "Min price must be a number." using `loc[-1]` mapped to a friendly label.
  - If `fetch` throws a TypeError, throw `new Error("Cannot reach the PaperDesk server. Check that it is still running.")`.
  - For a 500, say "Something went wrong on the server. The details are in the server window."
- (b) In `exchange.py`, replace the `{e}` messages with field-specific text, e.g. "Shares must be a number, for example 10." The order body's field names are known at the point of parsing.
- (c) Make `add_alert` (`exchange.py:~1139`, `parse_num`) and account creation (`server.py:~343`) catch the parse error and raise `ValueError("Price must be a number, for example 150.25.")` and `ValueError("Starting cash must be a number, for example 100000.")`.

**2. A stopped server or a dropped connection looks live.**
- When the server stops, the only signal is the 8 px dot turning red, with tooltip "Reconnecting…".
- Everything else stays on screen frozen as if live: "Market open · 04:39:58", all prices, the ticket.
- Fix:
  - When `connected` has been false for more than 3 s, show a full-width bar under the header: "Connection to the PaperDesk server lost. Prices and balances below are frozen at HH:MM:SS. Reconnecting…"
  - Grey out the price numbers (add a `stale` class to `.app`).
  - Disable "Review" with the title "Waiting for the server to reconnect".
  - On reconnect, show a 3 s toast: "Reconnected. Prices are live again."
  - Phone: the same bar under `.m-top`.

**3. Background events are invisible: margin calls, liquidations, dividends and splits are never shown.**
- `lifecycle/manager.py` emits `{"type":"lifecycle", kind, message}` events and keeps an account log at GET /api/accounts/{id}/lifecycle.
- `App.tsx:96-102` handles only `alert`, `fill` and `order` events, so these messages are dropped:
  - "Margin call: equity … (deficit …)"
  - "Margin call liquidation: sell 10 MKTX at market…"
  - "Margin call met"
  - "Margin checks paused"
- Liquidation orders have `role="liquidation"` and no `parent_id`, so a liquidation fill produces no toast. Positions simply vanish.
- The liquidation time appears only inside the Settings dialog (`Settings.tsx:73-74`), not on the main screen.
- Fix:
  - In the `onmessage` handler, add `if (e.type === "lifecycle" && e.account_id === subRef.current.accountId) notify(e.message, ["margin_call","liquidation"].includes(e.kind))`.
  - Extend `marginNotice` (`App.tsx:180`) to include the deficit and `liquidation_at`. Exact text is in the wording table, row W1.
  - Add a small "Account events" list, which can be a section in the Activity tab, reading /lifecycle.

**4. At 1280x720 the chart covers the bottom tab bar.**
- Measured at 1280x720:
  - `.main-view` bottom = 450.8
  - `.chart` bottom = 476.5
  - `.bottom` top = 456.8
- The Positions / Orders / … tab buttons are hidden under the chart's time axis.
- Cause: `styles.css:69` `.chart { min-height: 220px }` plus the quote header and chart bar exceed the `.main-view` flex share, and `.main-view` does not clip.
- Fix: in `styles.css`, add `.main-view { overflow: hidden; }` and change `.chart` to `min-height: 160px`. Better, also `@media (max-height: 760px) { .main-view { flex-basis: 62%; } .bottom { min-height: 170px; } }`.
- Recheck 1280x720 and 1366x768.

**5. Between 900 and 1100 px wide (including 1024x640) the layout falls apart.**
- `styles.css:118-124` stacks everything in one column at 1024 px. The page is 2849 px tall.
  - The watchlist stretches across 1000 px.
  - The order ticket is about 1180 px down.
  - Depth bars span the full width.
- Clicking "Close…" on a position seeds the ticket but does not scroll to it, so nothing visibly happens.
- Fix: for 900–1100 px use two columns, `grid-template-columns: 220px minmax(0,1fr)`, with the right column (ticket and depth) placed under the watchlist in column 1. Or move the ticket above the depth panel and directly after `.main-view`.
- Either way, `closePosition` and `tradeOption` (`App.tsx:147-156`) should call `document.querySelector('.ticket')?.scrollIntoView({block:'nearest'})` and focus the Shares input.

**6. The order preview does not make cost and risk clear.**
Seen in the screenshots:
- Options:
  - "Estimated price 75.40" with "Estimated cost $7,540.65". The ×100 multiplier is never stated in the preview.
  - Writing a put shows no secured-cash amount or assignment explanation.
- Limit orders: a limit at 400 with the ask at 454.65 shows "Position after 15" as if it would fill now.
- Brackets: the preview never mentions the take-profit and stop-loss legs or what each would gain or lose.
- Closing and reversing: selling 100 while holding 15 is labelled "Review: Sell", with no mention that 85 shares will be shorted.
- Prices in the preview have no "$", while cost does.

Fix in `Ticket.tsx:135-144`:
- Option rows: "Price per share $75.40 × 100 shares per contract".
- Limit away from the market: a line "Fills only at $400.00 or better. The current ask is $454.65, so this order will wait."
- Brackets: "Take profit at $500.00: about +$228.05. Stop loss at $420.00: about −$171.95."
- Sell qty greater than position: change the side label to "Sell 15 and short 85". Add a preview warning: "This sells your 15 shares and opens a short position of 85 shares."
- Short options: "Cash set aside to secure this put: $X" (margin accounts: "Margin required: $X").
- Use `usd()` for every money value.

**7. The 900 px phone layout hides key columns and the review button.**
- Account screen (iPhone 13 portrait): the Positions table cuts off after Market value. Day P&L, Open P&L and the "Close…" button are off-screen to the right. The tab strip is cut at "Re…". Neither has a scroll cue.
- Chart screen: the quote grid ("Prev close", "Day r…") and the indicator buttons ("SM…") are clipped at the right edge with no cue.
- Keyboard emulation, Pixel 7 at 55% height: the nav is hidden, but "Review: Buy" sits below the fold. After review, only "Estimated price" is visible above the sticky Place button. Cost, fees and buying power after are hidden, so the user can place without seeing the cost.
- Keyboard emulation, iPhone 13 at 55% height: 390x365 matches `@media (orientation: landscape) and (max-height:540px)` (`mobile.css:207`). The UI flips to the side-nav landscape layout, the paper banner wraps one word per line in a 150 px-tall header, and the Review button is covered.
  - Because `index.html` sets `interactive-widget=resizes-content`, Android Chrome really does shrink the viewport like this when the keyboard opens.
- Fixes:
  - Phone positions: render cards instead of a table below 600 px. Each card shows the description, then "15 sh · avg $454.37 · mark $453.88", then "Open P&L −$7.60 (−0.11%)", then a full-width Close button.
  - Otherwise, pin the first column and the Close column (`position: sticky; right: 0`) and add a right-edge fade on `.scroll` when it overflows.
  - Make the `.m-kb` rules beat the landscape media query, e.g. `.m-kb.m-shell` overrides for `.m-top-row2 { display:none }`.
  - While `.m-kb` is set, make the ticket's action row `position: sticky; bottom: 0`.
  - Show a one-line cost summary inside the sticky bar: "Est. cost $1,361.55 · Place paper order".

**8. Rejections do not say how to fix them and are formatted inconsistently.**
- "This order would be rejected: insufficient buying power." gives no amounts.
- The PDT and settled-cash messages end in a doubled period. `Ticket.tsx:58` appends "." to reasons that already end with "." or ").", producing "…allowed again on 2026-11-29.." (I confirmed the reason text via the preview API).
- Amounts appear as "19,998.98" and "25,000.00" with no "$". Dates are ISO ("2026-11-29", "next settlement 2026-11-25").
- No link to the setting that controls the rule.
- Short selling in a cash account is offered as "Review: Sell short" and then rejected.
- Fixes:
  - `Ticket.tsx:58`: `setError(p.reason.replace(/\.?$/, "."))`, with the reason first-letter-capitalised and no "This order would be rejected:" prefix. Put the heading "This order can't be placed" in bold above it.
  - When the reason starts with "Pattern day trader" or "Cash accounts can only buy with settled cash", add a link button "Change this rule in Settings" that opens `SettingsDialog`. Pass an `openSettings` prop from App.
  - Backend `_fmt` (`lifecycle/manager.py`): prefix "$" and format dates as "Sun 29 Nov".
  - Insufficient buying power (`risk/margin.py:310`, surfaced via `exchange.py:715`): see W7.
  - Ticket in cash accounts with no position: label the sell button "Sell" and show the hint "You have no MKTX shares to sell. Cash accounts cannot sell short."

**9. Times have no time zone, and zones mix within one screen.**
In live mode:
- The header ("Market open · 04:28:25") and the chart axis use the browser's local time with no label (`util.ts:54`, `Chart.tsx:82`).
- Orders, Fills and Activity times are also local and unlabelled.
- Exports are UTC.
In replay:
- The bar shows "09:30:04 ET" and "14:30 your time" while the header next to it shows "Market open · 14:30:04".
- The Fills tab showed "Mar 13, 20:00:00" for a 16:00 ET expiry.
- The picker mixes "09:30–13:00 ET" with "closes early at 1:00 pm".
In "us" session mode (from code), "Pre-market"/"After hours" are ET concepts shown next to an unlabelled local clock.
Fixes:
- In `util.ts`, add a `zoneLabel` (`Intl.DateTimeFormat().resolvedOptions().timeZone` → short name) and use it once in the header: "Market open · 04:28:25 (your time, UTC)".
- During replay, format `time`/`dateTime` in America/New_York and suffix "ET" in the column headers ("Time (ET)"). Pass a mode flag or set a module-level zone from `status.replay`.
- Hide the header clock while replaying, since the replay bar already has one.
- Picker description: use 24 h consistently ("closes early at 13:00 ET").

**10. Destructive actions lack confirmation, and Settings loses edits silently.**
No confirmation on:
- "Cancel all open orders" (`Panels.tsx:78`), which also cancels bracket stop-losses.
- "Exit replay" (`Replay.tsx:159`), which discards the sandbox and its positions.
- "Restore defaults" in Settings (`Settings.tsx:46`). It saves immediately on the server, with no toast.
- Cancelling a single bracket leg, which leaves the position unprotected with no warning.

Other problems:
- Clicking the dark backdrop of Settings, New account or Reset closes the dialog and discards edits (`onClick={onClose}`).
- No dialog closes on Escape.
- The Reset dialog's destructive button is styled primary blue.

Fixes:
- Use `window.confirm` (already used for journal delete) or a small confirm modal:
  - Cancel all: "Cancel all 3 open orders? Stop-loss and take-profit orders protecting your positions are included."
  - Exit replay: "Exit the replay? The replay account and its positions and orders are discarded. Your paper accounts are not affected."
  - Restore defaults: "Restore the default rules for this account? Your changes to these settings are replaced." Then toast "Default rules restored".
- For a bracket child: "Cancel this stop loss? Your 5 MKTX shares will no longer be protected by it."
- Backdrop click: close only when nothing is dirty. Add an Escape handler.
- Add a `.danger` button style (red background, white text) for "Erase and reset".

## 4. Layout and visual issues by screen and viewport

**Desktop 1440x900**
- The header wraps to two rows: stats on row 1, status, account select and actions on row 2. That costs about 40 px.
  - Fix: move the stats into the header row and shrink the action buttons, or put actions in a "⋯" menu.
- The long account name widens the select to about 330 px. Give it `max-width: 220px; text-overflow: ellipsis` and a title.
- The TradingView attribution logo sits on top of the volume bars at the bottom-left of every chart. Keep the attribution but move it, e.g. `layout.attributionLogo:false` plus a small "Charts by TradingView" link in the chart bar, which satisfies the licence.
- The chart's first time-axis label is clipped ("24").
- The bottom panel is mostly empty space when there are few rows. The chart gets 58%; consider 65/35 when the bottom tab has fewer than 5 rows.
- Positions Day P&L disagrees with the header. A position opened today shows Day P&L measured from the previous close: +$390,000.00 on a 200,000-share position bought minutes earlier, while the header shows −$67,680.00. Compute position day P&L from the fill price for today's lots.
- Options chain:
  - It opens at the deepest in-the-money strike (340 with spot 454) and does not scroll to the money.
  - No spot marker row.
  - In-the-money shading (#161f2c on #12161c) is nearly invisible.
  - Fix: scroll the row nearest spot into view on load, insert a thin "Spot $454.09" divider row, and use #1a2a40 for in-the-money cells.
- The order ticket shows the underlying's depth ladder under an option ticket (MKTX prices around 454 under a 380 call). Hide depth when `isOption(seed.symbol)`, or label it "MKTX (underlying) depth".
- Research: the "unavailable" notice stretches to fill the whole panel as a huge yellow box. Fix: `.research { align-content: start; }` in Research.css.
- Toasts stack bottom-right over Time and sales and the depth ladder. Up to 4 toasts are stacked, at 5 s each (9 s for errors). For order results, also show a persistent inline confirmation in the ticket ("Filled: bought 10 MKTX at $454.36 · View in Positions"), cleared on the next edit.
- Panels built by different agents differ:
  - Settings uses inline styles and 11 px help text.
  - Reports uses its own `.rep-*` scale, with section headings inconsistent with `h3`.
  - Replay buttons are ghost buttons at 40 px.
  - Login is larger (15 px).
  - Mostly they read as one product (same tokens), but button heights vary (`.sm` 2 px padding in the top bar and Orders, 8 px in Settings and dialogs). Unify on two sizes.
- Reports in the bottom panel is cramped. Overview plus chart needs about 900 px, but the panel is about 330 px, so the chart is cut off. Add an "Open in full view" option, or let Reports take over the centre column.

- The chart overlaps the bottom tab bar (problem 4).

**1024x640 and 900–1100 px**
- Single-column layout: 2849 px tall page, full-width watchlist, ticket far below (problem 5).
- The quote header stacks oddly: the price is bottom-left and the stats grid sits above it in the centre.

## 7. What works well and should not be changed

- The paper-trading banner is always visible on desktop, phone and login. In replay it switches to a blue "Replay of Fri, Nov 28, 2025 · simulated market data · paper trading, no real money" banner, which is very clear.
- "simulated depth", "model prices, not market data" and "All figures use simulated prices." labelling. The Company notice explains fictional companies.
- The review-then-place flow:
  - Editing any field clears the preview.
  - The Place button is coloured by side.
  - The button says "Place paper order".
  - The market-order slippage warning.
  - Buying power after.
- Side labels that adapt to the position: "Buy to open", "Sell to close", "Buy to cover", "Sell short".
- Reset dialog text ("…It cannot be undone."), and the journal delete confirmation.
- Replay:
  - The picker explains the sandbox and that paper accounts are untouched.
  - "Start with $100,000.00" button.
  - The ET clock plus "your time".
  - Helpful refusals: "A replay cannot go back in time…", "The session ends at 13:00 New York time."
  - Speed segment and step buttons.
- Login: large targets, 16 px input (no iOS zoom), honest "not encrypted" note, clear wrong-passcode message, focus returned to the field, rate limiting.
- The PDT and settled-cash messages already name the cause, the numbers and when it clears. They only need "$", friendly dates and a single period.
- Settings: help text under every rule, dependent number fields dimmed when their toggle is off, and rules for the other account type hidden with a note.
- Phone shell:
  - Bottom nav with icons and labels.
  - Big Buy and Sell buttons beside the quote.
  - Calls / Puts / Both toggle.
  - 44 px targets.
  - The keyboard detector that hides the nav (it needs only the fixes in problem 7).
- Mobile landscape side-nav.
- Gains notes explaining long-term and wash-sale rules honestly ("this report is not tax advice").
- Empty states in Positions, Fills, Gains and Journal are written as plain sentences that say what will appear.
